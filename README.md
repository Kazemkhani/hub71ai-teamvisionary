# Landing Sequence

The critical path for moving a company and its people to Abu Dhabi.

Abu Dhabi issues a trade licence in about 6 minutes and a work visa in about 5 days, yet a relocating company is typically not operational for 2 to 3 months, because the steps across government, banks, landlords and schools are done in the wrong order and nobody shows the dependencies. Landing Sequence builds the dependency graph for the company and every relocating person, computes the critical path and the go-live date, scores bank-account readiness, surfaces hidden obligations in dirhams, and recomputes instantly when you change a decision.

Built for the Hub71+ AI Hackathon, 2 October 2026. Team: Team Visionary.
